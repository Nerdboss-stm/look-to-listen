# DEMO.md: the pitch (v3, room format)

Round 1 is in one of 6 rooms, about 15 teams back to back. Then the final, in front of everyone.
Judges remember one moment and one line. The pitch is built around live moments, not slides.

## Roles
- Saran: presents. Holds the backpack. Borrows the object.
- Hands: teammate, sleep mask on, one earbud for the guidance words, seated at the board.
- Operator (optional): plugs in the projector, then hands off the laptop.

## Setup in the room (60 s, rehearsed)
1. HDMI into the projector, mirror the display.
2. Board on the table, phone already on the mast, app already running.
3. Hands sits down, earbud in, mask in hand.
4. First label visible on the projector. Go.

## The room run (3:00)
| Time | What happens (live) | Saran says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Saran tips his backpack onto the board. Hands pulls on the mask. Labels appear on every object. | Nothing for 2 s. Let the sound and the labels land. |
| 0:03 | AGENT: reads the real flight email, fetches tsa.gov, writes checks. | "That's everything in my bag. I fly to Vegas on Sunday. She can't see. Gemini can." |
| 0:12 | Trace keeps streaming. | The hook line (see the two openings below). |
| 0:30 | Hook decision on screen. Plan released. | "It couldn't release that plan until its own tests passed. Now it can." |
| 0:35 | Tone guides the hands. Hydrogen names each grab. Labels read on hold-up. A rule break: STOP before it lands. Hands keeps packing while Saran talks. | Only lines that match the screen. When STOP fires, stop talking. |
| 1:30 | Saran borrows an object from a judge's table (or picks one up in the room) and drops it on the board. | "May I borrow this? We've never seen it. Neither has Gemini." |
| 1:40 | The object is labeled, judged against the rules, given a zone. | Read what the screen says. |
| 1:55 | Second errand on the same pile. New plan in seconds, marked "unverified" until its checks land. | "Change of plan. Gym tonight, not Vegas." (Or a judge's call, if one offers.) "Same pile. Different job." |
| 2:15 | "Leave for EWR" appears in the real calendar after "yes". | "And the last step goes back to a screen, after she says yes." |
| 2:25 | END CARD: today's runs, raw vs Offscreen counts. | "Same model, same camera, no harness: [n] of [n]. With Offscreen: [n] of [n]." |
| 2:40 | Close. | Close (below). |

## The hook line (pick by who is in the room)
- Engineers in the room (DeepMind, Google, MIT, Agora): "Gemini 4 Hydrogen sees every object and her hands, live. A Managed Agent read my email and TSA's rules and wrote a check for every step as code. Every agent this year stops at the screen. This is Offscreen."
- Investors and founders in the room: "Google made an agent that pays your bills free on Tuesday. Agents already hire people for physical work: RentAHuman signed up 500,000 people. Those agents send instructions and wait for a photo. Nothing watches the work. This is Offscreen."
- Unknown room: use the engineer line, then the RentAHuman sentence in the close.

## Close (under 15 s)
"Every agent this year stops at the screen. Ours finished the job on this table, and proved every step. Today it's her hands. The same plan runs a robot arm. Offscreen."

## The final showdown (if we advance)
- Same run. Bigger screen, so the labels and STOP read from the back.
- Borrow the object from the front row or the MC.
- Optional crowd moment for the second errand: "Where should she pack for next?" Take the first answer that is a moving-things errand. If nobody answers, use "gym tonight".
- Lead with the investor hook line. The final crowd is mixed.

## Rules for the run
- Never tidy the bag before a run. Never pick the borrowed object in advance.
- No staged mistakes. Zero stops is fine.
- If the compile passes 45 s, the fast path runs labeled "unverified". Say so out loud.
- If the agent says "can't do" for the second errand, read its reason and pick another. That builds trust.
- Never say a number that is not on screen.

## Q&A (one breath each, then stop talking)
- Is this staged? That's my real bag, my real flight, and your object. Hand me anything else.
- How is this different from Tasks in Gemini? Tasks finishes errands in apps. Offscreen finishes the physical part and proves each step, on the same Hydrogen model.
- How is this different from RentAHuman? They find the person. We watch the work.
- Astra's bike demo did this. In that 2025 video the person did the work and checked it himself. Offscreen plans, watches every step, and stops mistakes before they land.
- Why the blindfold? So every success belongs to the model.
- What does the Managed Agent do that a script can't? It reads rules it has never seen, writes the checks as code, tests them on this camera, and cannot release until its tests pass. Every 30 minutes it learns from new runs, and a second hook stops it from breaking old cases.
- What can't it do? Folding, tying, assembling, cooking. It says so.
- How fast? The loop runs at 30 fps on the laptop. Hydrogen p50 and compile time are on screen.

## Pitch practice (from tonight)
- Learn the 2 s silence, the first line and the close by heart. Everything else follows the screen.
- Record one full run on your phone tonight. Watch it once. Cut anything that isn't on screen.
- Saturday: 3 mock-room runs with a timer, the setup included, and a teammate asking the Q&A cold.

## Insurance
A 60 s recording of the best run of the day at T minus 60, labeled with its time. Play it only if the live run cannot start.
