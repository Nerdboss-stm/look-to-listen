# DEMO.md: Eyes Closed, the pitch

Round 1: one of 6 rooms, about 15 teams back to back. Then the final, in front of everyone.
Saran gives the whole pitch blindfolded and is the hands. Nothing on the table is arranged.

## Roles
- Saran: presents blindfolded and does the errand.
- Operator (teammate, if any): plugs in the projector, then hands off. Takes over only for a fallback.

## Setup in the room (60 s, eyes open)
1. HDMI in, mirror the display.
2. Board on the table, phone on the mast, app running.
3. Earbud in. Mask in hand. Backpack zipped at your feet.
4. First label visible on the projector. Mask on. Go.

## Hook lines (pick by who is in the room)
- DeepMind or robotics judges: "Your robotics team says knowing when a task is done is one of the hardest problems in robotics. Hydrogen is its eyes, Neon its brain, the Decisions API its reflexes, and a Managed Agent writes and tests its rules. That's how I can bet my eyes on it."
- Other engineers: "Hydrogen sees every object and my hands, live. Neon read my email and TSA's rules and wrote a check for every step. A Managed Agent couldn't release this plan until its own tests passed."
- Investors and founders: "Google made an agent that pays your bills free on Tuesday. Agents already hire people for physical work: RentAHuman reports 500,000 users. Those agents wait for a photo. Nothing watches the work. This does."
- Unknown room: the DeepMind line.

## Close (under 15 s)
"Every agent this year stops at the screen. This one works with your eyes closed. Eyes Closed."

## The final showdown
- Same run, bigger screen.
- The mystery object from the front row or the MC.
- The second errand from the crowd: "Where should I pack for next?" Take the first errand that is about moving things.
- Open with the investor hook. Put the DeepMind line in the close.

## Rules for the run
- The mask stays on until the END CARD.
- Never tidy the bag. Never arrange the mystery object.
- No staged mistakes. Zero stops is fine.
- If tracking drops: lift the mask, say where it broke and what we changed, and finish sighted.
- If the agent says "can't do" for the second errand: read its reason aloud and take another.
- Never say a number that is not on screen.

## Q&A (one breath each)
- Is this staged? My bag, my flight, your object. Hand me anything else.
- How do we know you didn't peek? Check the mask.
- What's new? Knowing a step is done, live, on things it has never seen, with no human checking. DeepMind's robotics team calls that one of the hardest problems in robotics.
- How is this different from Tasks in Gemini? Tasks stops at the screen. This finishes the physical part and proves each step.
- How is this different from RentAHuman? They find the person. We watch the work.
- Astra's bike demo? The person did the work and checked it himself. Here nobody checks but the model.
- Why these models? Hydrogen is fast eyes. Neon reasons about rules and writes the checks. Decision 1 gives a calibrated yes or no in about 0.2 s.
- What does the Managed Agent do that a script can't? It reads rules it has never seen, writes the checks as code, tests them on this camera, and can't release until they pass. Every 30 minutes it learns from new runs, and a second hook stops it from breaking old cases.
- What's the baseline? One Hydrogen call for plans, and raw Hydrogen live for guiding. Same inputs.
- Where does it fail? The failure line on the END CARD.
- What can't it do? Folding, tying, assembling, cooking. It says so.
