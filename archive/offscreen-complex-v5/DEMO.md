# DEMO.md: Offscreen, the pitch

Round 1: one of 6 rooms, about 15 teams back to back. Then the final, in front of everyone.
The pitch is built from live moments. No slides.

## Roles
- Saran: presents and does the physical job.
- Operator (teammate, if any): plugs in the projector, then hands off. Takes over only for a fallback.

## Setup in the room (60 s)
1. HDMI in, mirror the display.
2. Board on the table, phone on the mast, app running, TIMELINE visible.
3. Earbud in. Backpack zipped at your feet.
4. First label on the projector. Go.

## The room run (3:00)
| Time | What happens (live) | Saran says |

| 0:00 | TIMELINE: the whole day of our table. | "Every agent this year stops at the screen. Ours has been watching our table since ten this morning." |

| 2:45 | | Close (below). |

## Hook lines (pick by who is in the room)
- DeepMind or Google engineers: "Hydrogen sees, Argon remembers nine hours, Neon plans, the Decisions API checks every step, and a Managed Agent runs the whole job. Your models, off the screen."
- Other engineers: "Two cameras, a microphone and a whole day of footage, queried in seconds, then turned into a plan a person can do with their hands."
- Investors and founders: "Google made an agent that pays your bills free on Tuesday. Muse and Instinct shop through Shopify. All of them stop at the screen. Ours sees your world, remembers it, and works in it."
- Unknown room: the DeepMind line.

## Close (under 15 s)
"Every agent this year stops at the screen. Ours sees your world, remembers it, works in it through your hands, and buys what's missing. Offscreen."

## The final showdown
- Same run, bigger screen. Take the first memory question from the crowd.
- Open with the investor hook. Put the DeepMind line in the close.

## Rules for the run
- Never pick the memory question if a judge offers one.
- Never tidy the bag. Never hide an item to create the wallet moment.
- No staged mistakes. Zero stops is fine.
- If Argon is slow: the answer comes from the log, marked "log only". Say so.
- If something breaks: say where it broke and what we changed. The brief asks for that.
- Never say a number that is not on screen.

## Q&A (one breath each)
- Is this staged? The questions are yours. Ask another one.
- How does the memory work? Every change on the table becomes an event with a clip. Neon finds the right events, Argon checks the clips.
- Why not put the whole day in one model call? We tried that as the baseline. It's on the END CARD.
- How far before it breaks? On the END CARD, by hours of footage.
- What does the Managed Agent do? It runs the job through our tools. One hook blocks the plan until its checks pass. Another blocks spending until you say yes. Every 30 minutes it learns from new runs.
- How is this different from Tasks in Gemini, Muse or Duet? They stop at the screen. This one sees, remembers and works in the room.
- How is this different from glasses assistants? They answer from one moment. This answers from the whole day, shows the clip, then does the job.
- Privacy? Only our table and team on camera. Nobody else is recorded or transcribed.
- What can't it do? Folding, tying, assembling, cooking. It says so.

## Practice
- 10 full runs on different bags and goals, 8 clean (AT14). 3 of them in a mock room with a timer and a teammate asking the Q&A cold.

## Insurance
A 60 s recording of the best run at T minus 60, labeled with its time. Play it only if the live run cannot start.
